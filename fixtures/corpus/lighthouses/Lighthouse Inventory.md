# Lighthouse Inventory

The lights of the fictional Harrow Coast, south to north. Character notation is explained in
`Lighthouses.md` §4; range caveats in §3.

## 1. Landfall lights

- **Carrick Head** — Fl(2) 20s, 58 m, nominal range 24 M; first-order Fresnel on a mercury bath; the primary landfall light for the whole coast.
- **Stonepoint** — Fl 15s, 41 m, nominal range 21 M; second-order; sited low deliberately, as the headland above it is under cloud most mornings (`Lighthouses.md` §3.2).
- **Mull Rock** — Fl(3) 30s, 34 m, nominal range 18 M; rock station, relieved by sea; the station most often unreachable in winter.

## 2. Harbour and channel lights

- **Harrow Inner** — Oc 4s, 11 m, nominal range 9 M; sectored, white 187°–194°, red to the west, green to the east; the channel light for the main approach.
- **Harrow Outer** — Iso 2s, 8 m, nominal range 7 M; forms a transit with Harrow Inner when both bear 190°, which is the recommended approach (`../navigation/Navigation.md` §4.1).
- **Fisher Quay** — F red, 6 m, nominal range 4 M; marks the quay head only; no navigational significance beyond the basin.
- **Netherby** — Al white/red 6s, 9 m, nominal range 8 M; alternating character chosen because Fisher Quay's fixed red is visible on the same bearing.

## 3. Minor and discontinued

- **Cowl Island** — Fl 5s, 14 m, nominal range 10 M; sixth-order reflector optic, the last reflector still in service on this coast.
- **Bray Ness** — discontinued; tower standing and charted as a daymark; shows no light and appears on older charts as Fl 10s, which is a trap on an uncorrected chart.

## 4. Notes

Every range above is nominal. In haze apply the reduction in `Lighthouses.md` §3.1, noting
that the figure is disputed (`../navigation/Study Plan.md` §2).
