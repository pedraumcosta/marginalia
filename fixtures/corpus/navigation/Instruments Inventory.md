# Instruments Inventory

One line per instrument. Prose and framings live in `Navigation.md`; this is a catalogue.

## 1. Angle-measuring

- **Cross-staff** — earliest practical altitude instrument; observer sights horizon and body simultaneously; requires looking near the Sun, which cost many navigators their eyesight.
- **Backstaff** — solves that by observing the Sun's shadow with the body behind the observer; accurate to roughly 2 minutes of arc in trained hands.
- **Octant** — 45° arc, doubled by reflection to 90°; the first instrument using the double-reflection principle.
- **Sextant** — 60° arc reading to 120°; vernier or micrometer drum; best practical accuracy about 0.2 minutes of arc. See `Navigation.md` §2.1.
- **Bubble sextant** — artificial horizon for use where no sea horizon is visible; markedly less accurate, roughly 10 minutes of arc, because the bubble responds to the vessel's motion.

## 2. Timekeeping

- **Marine chronometer** — spring-driven, temperature-compensated; rated rather than reset, so its daily gain is known and applied. Made lunar distance obsolete (`Navigation.md` §2.2).
- **Deck watch** — carried to the observation point so the chronometer need not leave its gimbals.
- **Sandglass** — used with the log line for speed; a 28-second glass pairs with a line knotted every 47 feet 3 inches.

## 3. Direction

- **Dry-card compass** — pivoted card, badly affected by vessel motion.
- **Liquid-filled compass** — damped by fluid; the standard steering compass.
- **Azimuth ring** — fitted over a compass to take bearings of celestial bodies, used to check deviation (`Navigation.md` §5.3).
- **Pelorus** — a dumb compass card with no magnetic element, for relative bearings.

## 4. Depth and speed

- **Hand lead** — 7 lb lead on a marked line; armed with tallow to sample the bottom.
- **Deep-sea lead** — 30 lb or more for offshore soundings; needs the vessel stopped.
- **Chip log** — wooden quadrant streamed on a knotted line, timed with a sandglass.
- **Patent log** — towed rotator driving a mechanical counter; continuous, and fouls readily in weed.

## 5. Not held

Gyrocompass, echo sounder, radio direction finder. Out of period for this collection.
